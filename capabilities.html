The Firm

Artist-First execution meets senior judgment.

Icon Breaking Agency is a modern artist-development and release-infrastructure company. We give artists the full structure of a label team, providing creative direction, marketing strategy, positioning, and a long-term roadmap. We believe in artists being supported by teams that don't require or depend on a label, or standard industry timelines and network constraints.

Founded in 2018 as an entertainment branding and marketing agency, IBA serves recording artists, industry professionals, reality-TV talent, and actors.
